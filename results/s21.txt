Three separable outcomes: the direct model is correct but context-bounded (overflows past its window at
scale 300); the naive value-keyed union scales but is silently wrong (recall 0 even at scale 20 — it
reconciles every disagreement away); only the metric-keyed merge() is both correct and scale-free.
